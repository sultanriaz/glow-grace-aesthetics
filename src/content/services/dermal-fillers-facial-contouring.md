## Overview

As the face undergoes the natural process of chronological aging, volume depletion occurs across three fundamental layers: deep structural bone reabsorbs, facial fat compartments deflate and shift downward, and dermal collagen loses elasticity. This structural shift frequently leads to hollowing in the temples, flattening of the cheekbones, deepening of smile lines (nasolabial folds), and soft jowling along the lower jaw. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our doctor-led facial contouring treatments aim to restore balanced architectural support without surgery.

We reject the generic 'over-plumped' look that has given cosmetic fillers an unfortunate reputation. Instead, our medical team approaches facial contouring from an anatomical perspective. By placing micro-aliquots of resilient, cross-linked hyaluronic acid directly onto deep anatomical anchor points, we provide subtle mechanical lift to descending soft tissue. This gently restores youthful light reflection across the mid-face, sharpens the jawline margin, and softens shadow lines—leaving your face looking naturally rested, proportioned, and contoured.

## How it works

The treatment utilizes high-viscosity, biocompatible hyaluronic acid gels with high lifting capacity (G-prime). Administered through fine needles or flexible, blunt-tip safety micro-cannulas, the gel is deposited along deep supra-periosteal planes or within deflated deep fat pads. The cohesive gel acts as an internal scaffold, physically supporting overlying tissue layers, lifting sunken contours, and drawing microscopic moisture into the extracellular matrix to improve dermal hydration.

## What it can help with

- Flattened or deflated cheekbones and mid-face hollowing
- Softening deep nasolabial folds (smile lines) and marionette grooves
- Weak, recessed chin profiles and softening jawline borders
- Sunken temple hollowing that creates an angular, aged facial silhouette
- Restoring harmonious facial symmetry and balanced light reflection
