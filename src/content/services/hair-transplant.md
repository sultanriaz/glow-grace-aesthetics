## Overview

Hair transplant surgery at Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan is designed for men and women experiencing significant hair thinning, pattern baldness, or receding hairlines. Experiencing progressive hair loss can affect self-confidence, professional presentation, and emotional well-being. Rather than relying on temporary concealers or unverified remedies, follicular transplantation offers a structured, surgical approach to restoring density.

Every surgical plan begins with an in-depth clinical consultation. Our doctor assesses the stability of your donor area, the progression rate of your loss, and the structural symmetry of your face. By prioritising conservative graft management, we preserve donor reserves for potential future needs while restoring definition to the frontal hairline or crown. The procedure is performed in a clean, clinical setting using modern micro-surgical tools designed to protect follicular viability and support smooth, predictable tissue recovery.

## How it works

The procedure utilises Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE). Under local anaesthesia, tiny micro-punches gently detach individual follicular units containing one to four hairs from the donor zone. The harvested grafts are inspected, counted, and stored in a chilled holding solution to maintain cellular viability. The doctor then creates micro-incisions in the recipient area, carefully orienting each angle and depth to mimic the natural swirl and direction of your existing hair before gently placing each graft.

## What it can help with

- Receding frontal hairlines and thinning temporal angles
- Male and female pattern androgenetic alopecia
- Thinning crown and mid-scalp density
- Restoring facial symmetry and youthful hairline framing
- Long-term follicular coverage in donor-stable candidates
