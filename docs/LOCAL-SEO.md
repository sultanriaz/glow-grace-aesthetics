## 3. Ethical Patient Review Protocol (PHC & PMDC Compliant)

Positive, authentic patient reviews on Google Maps build profound local trust in Southern Punjab:

### Ethical Best Practices
- **Ask In-Person After Successful Follow-Ups:** When a patient expresses satisfaction during their post-procedure review, politely hand them an appointment card with the Google Review QR code.
- **WhatsApp Follow-up Message:** Send a simple, respectful post-care message:
  > *"Assalam o Alaikum [Name], we hope your recovery is progressing comfortably. If you felt well-cared for at Glow & Grace Aesthetics, we would deeply appreciate your honest feedback on our Google profile: [Google Review Shortlink]. Thank you for placing your trust in us!"*
- **Respond to Every Review Professionally:** Thank patients courteously. If addressing a concern, maintain medical confidentiality and invite them to discuss their situation privately at the clinic.

### Prohibited Practices
- **NEVER Buy or Post Fake Reviews:** Google's algorithms detect Pakistani review rings and will permanently blacklist or suspend the listing.
- **NEVER Incentivize Reviews:** Do not offer discounts or free treatments in exchange for reviews (violates Google guidelines and PHC advertising ethics).
