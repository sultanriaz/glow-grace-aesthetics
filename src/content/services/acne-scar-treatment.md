## Overview

Acne is more than a passing cosmetic issue; severe or persistent breakouts can leave profound physical scarring and emotional distress. In South Asia, cystic acne frequently leaves a mixture of indented dermal scars and persistent brown post-inflammatory staining. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our doctor-guided acne and scar programme takes a methodical, evidence-grounded approach to restoring skin smoothness and self-esteem.

We recognise that effective scar revision requires a dual strategy. First, ongoing bacterial activity and sebaceous inflammation must be controlled to halt the creation of fresh blemishes. Second, mature indented scars—including rolling, boxcar, and tethered depressions—are released and stimulated using targeted clinical techniques like subcision, medical microneedling, and gentle resurfacing peels. Rather than masking textural flaws, we encourage your skin to synthesise healthy new collagen, gradually raising pitted depressions and restoring a calm, balanced surface.

## How it works

Treatment is tailored to the specific scar morphology present. For tethered rolling scars, micro-subcision frees deep fibrous bands anchoring the skin down to deeper fascial planes. Concurrently, medical microneedling creates micro-trauma within the scar bed, stimulating dermal fibroblasts to deposit organised type I and type III collagen. Gentle chemical resurfacing accelerates epidermal cell turnover, fading post-acne dark spots while refining the overall superficial skin contour.

## What it can help with

- Depressed rolling and boxcar scars from past cystic or pustular acne
- Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (stubborn dark red and brown marks)
- Active papular and comedonal acne requiring medical regulation
- Uneven, coarse surface texture and enlarged sebaceous pores
- Restoring skin elasticity and healthy dermal thickness across the cheeks and temples
