## Overview

Sudden or progressive hair shedding can be distressing, leaving pillows, shower drains, and hairbrushes filled with strands. In South Asian climates, stress, postpartum hormonal shifts, water hardness, and nutritional deficiencies frequently accelerate hair loss. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our hair fall management programme moves beyond generic salon treatments by investigating the biological root causes of follicular weakness.

Rather than offering one-size-fits-all remedies, our medical team evaluates your scalp health, hair diameter variation, and systemic wellness factors. Early clinical intervention is crucial because miniaturised follicles are significantly easier to salvage before they progress to complete dormancy. By combining in-clinic micro-therapies with supportive home routines, we help rebalance the scalp microbiome, calm micro-inflammation, and nourish fragile roots. Our compassionate clinical environment ensures you receive honest advice, clear expectations, and ongoing support throughout your hair recovery journey.

## How it works

The treatment delivers bio-active peptides, amino acids, vitamins, and circulatory stimulants directly to the follicular bulb. Using superficial microneedling or gentle transdermal delivery, micro-channels are created across the scalp without harming surrounding tissue. This stimulates local microcirculation, increases nutrient oxygenation to sluggish roots, and extends the active anagen (growth) phase of the hair cycle while reducing early entry into the telogen (shedding) phase.

## What it can help with

- Telogen effluvium triggered by acute stress, fever, or weight changes
- Early-stage androgenetic alopecia in men and women
- Postpartum hair shedding and seasonal thinning
- Diffuse crown thinning and widening hair parting
- Dry, irritated, or nutritionally depleted scalp environments
