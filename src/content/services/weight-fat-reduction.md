## Overview

Many men and women maintain healthy diets and active physical routines yet find certain stubborn fat pockets virtually impossible to shift. Genetic predisposition frequently dictates where our bodies store stubborn subcutaneous fat—most commonly under the chin (submental fullness), around the flanks (love handles), on the lower abdomen, or across the inner thighs. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our non-surgical body contouring protocols provide a targeted medical pathway to refine these persistent problem areas.

We believe in responsible, evidence-guided aesthetics. We never promise magical overnight transformations or claim that non-invasive contouring replaces a balanced diet. Instead, our doctor evaluates your body proportions, skin elasticity, and subcutaneous tissue thickness to recommend safe, targeted modalities. By combining advanced radiofrequency heating, acoustic cavitation, and clinical lipolytic mesotherapy, we help sculpt and tighten your silhouette gradually, offering a dignified, non-surgical alternative to invasive liposuction.

## How it works

The treatment disrupts subcutaneous adipocyte membranes using focused thermal or acoustic energy, or localized lipolytic enzymes. In radiofrequency and cavitation protocols, controlled therapeutic temperatures induce apoptosis (programmed cell death) within localized fat cells while tightening surrounding dermal collagen fibres. In lipolytic mesotherapy, purified bile salts break down the adipocyte wall. The released lipid contents are slowly transported through the lymphatic channels to the liver, where they are naturally metabolized.

## What it can help with

- Submental fullness (double chin) resistant to diet and exercise
- Localised lower abdominal fat pouches following childbearing or weight changes
- Flank fullness ('love handles') that softens the natural waistline contour
- Localised fat bulges on the inner thighs, upper arms, or bra line
- Refining overall body contours and supporting long-term wellness confidence
