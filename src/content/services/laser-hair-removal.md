Laser hair removal at Glow & Grace Aesthetics utilizes clinical-grade optical devices designed to target melanin pigments within active-growth hair follicles through selective photothermolysis. The emitted light energy converts into controlled thermal energy, damaging follicular germ cells to impede future hair regrowth without harming surrounding epidermal tissues.

Equipped with continuous surface contact cooling, our lasers maximize comfort while ensuring safety across darker Fitzpatrick skin types common in our region. Over a planned treatment sequence, hair density is substantially reduced, and troublesome ingrown hairs and shaving bumps are eliminated. A mandatory patch test and doctor consultation precede all treatments to configure wavelength, fluence, and pulse width safely for your skin.
