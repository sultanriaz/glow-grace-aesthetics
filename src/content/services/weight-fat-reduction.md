Non-surgical fat reduction and body contouring at Glow & Grace Aesthetics are designed to address localized pockets of subcutaneous fat that persist despite consistent diet and physical exercise. Utilizing clinical technologies such as cavitation, radiofrequency lipolysis, or localized lipolytic mesotherapy, treatments target fat cell membranes to facilitate natural lymphatic clearance over time.

These procedures are intended for body sculpting rather than massive weight loss, serving as a refining adjunct to healthy living. We promote honest, medically grounded expectations rather than rapid transformation promises. A mandatory medical consultation with the doctor is conducted to evaluate tissue distribution, assess body composition, and determine whether non-surgical contouring matches your personal health goals.
