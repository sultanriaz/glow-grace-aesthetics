PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy for hair restoration harnesses the biological power of your body's own natural growth factors to support follicle vitality. A small sample of your blood is drawn and centrifuged under sterile laboratory conditions to isolate concentrated platelets rich in signaling proteins. 

This bioactive plasma is then carefully administered across thinning zones of the scalp to nourish dormant follicles and support microcirculation. The treatment aims to strengthen existing strands and encourage healthy hair growth cycles. Because autologous platelet concentrations and cellular responses vary between individuals, an initial consultation with the doctor is necessary to assess whether PRP aligns with your degree of thinning.
