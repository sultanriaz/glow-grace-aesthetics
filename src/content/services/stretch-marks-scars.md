## Overview

Stretch marks (striae distensae) and body scars are widespread dermatological concerns that frequently arise following rapid growth spurts during adolescence, substantial weight changes, bodybuilding, or pregnancy. When skin expands faster than the underlying structural scaffolding can accommodate, the collagen and elastin matrix in the reticular dermis ruptures. This leaves linear, slightly depressed bands that feel thinner and softer than healthy neighbouring skin.

At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our approach to stretch mark and scar revision is grounded in honesty and clinical science. We do not make false promises of effortless erasure; instead, we utilise evidence-guided collagen-induction therapies designed to rebuild dermal density from within. By pairing automated medical microneedling with restorative growth factor infusions, we stimulate fresh collagen synthesis inside the scar troughs. Over time, the treated marks become shallower, firmer, and more harmonious in colour with your natural skin tone.

## How it works

The treatment creates microscopic puncture channels directly through the fibrotic bands of the stretch mark. This controlled mechanical disruption triggers a localized healing response, recruiting platelets, cytokines, and fibroblasts to the damaged dermal layer. Over subsequent weeks, the fibroblasts deposit fresh, organised collagen and elastin fibres within the tear. Concurrently, topical regenerative factors penetrate deeply, stimulating local microcirculation and helping repigment pale, atrophic bands.

## What it can help with

- Post-pregnancy abdominal stretch marks and mild skin laxity
- Linear stretch marks on the hips, thighs, glutes, and upper arms
- Atrophic or slightly depressed surgical scars following minor procedures
- Rough, uneven textural scarring and thinned skin bands
- Restoring skin elasticity, dermal thickness, and tactile firmness
