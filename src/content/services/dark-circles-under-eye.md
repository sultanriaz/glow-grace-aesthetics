## Overview

The delicate skin beneath the eyes is the thinnest on the entire human body, measuring barely 0.5 mm in thickness. In South Asian genetic profiles, hereditary pigmentation, prolonged screen time, allergic rhinitis, and busy lifestyles frequently combine to create persistent dark circles and a perpetually fatigued appearance. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, we provide tailored medical under-eye care that moves far beyond superficial cosmetic concealers.

Successful under-eye rejuvenation starts with precise clinical diagnosis. Our doctor evaluates whether your dark circles are caused by dermal melanin deposits, sluggish venous pooling, or structural tear-trough depressions casting shadows. Once the primary cause is identified, we employ conservative, periorbital-safe interventions—such as bio-peptide mesotherapy, autologous platelet micro-infusions, or delicate micro-cannula volume restoration. By respecting the intricate anatomy of the eye area, we help soften dark shadows, hydrate crepey skin, and restore a naturally rested, refreshed gaze.

## How it works

Depending on the underlying cause, the treatment works through targeted mechanisms. For vascular and pigmentary circles, micro-infusions of biomimetic peptides, vitamin C, and caffeine stimulate microcirculation, reduce capillary leakage, and inhibit local melanin deposition. For anatomical tear-trough hollows, tiny amounts of soft, cohesive hyaluronic acid gel are placed beneath the orbicularis muscle using a blunt cannula, lifting the shadow and creating a smooth, light-reflective transition between the lower eyelid and cheek.

## What it can help with

- Tired, darkened periorbital rings that do not resolve with sleep
- Sunken tear-trough hollows casting shadows beneath the eyes
- Fine, crepey dehydration lines across the lower eyelid contour
- Sluggish periorbital microcirculation and morning puffiness
- Restoring a rested, vibrant, and youthful appearance to the upper face
