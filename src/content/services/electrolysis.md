## Overview

While modern laser technology offers rapid hair reduction across broad body areas, it relies on dark melanin pigment inside the hair shaft to generate heat. As a result, optical lasers cannot effectively treat grey, white, blonde, or very fine light-coloured hairs. For individuals with scattered, coarse white chin hairs, unpigmented hormonal strands, or delicate eyebrow shaping needs, electrolysis remains the definitive clinical modality for targeted follicle destruction.

At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our electrolysis treatments are performed using state-of-the-art computerized epilators and sterile, disposable micro-filaments. Unlike plucking or threading—which merely tears the hair shaft away and often stimulates the root to grow back thicker—electrolysis targets the germinative cells at the follicular base. By disabling the blood supply and matrix cells of each individual strand, it halts future regeneration. Our gentle, patient-focused approach ensures discrete facial hairs are eliminated with clinical precision, comfort, and safety.

## How it works

The practitioner slides an ultra-fine, insulated surgical filament directly into the natural opening of the hair follicle without piercing the skin surface. A controlled micro-pulse of high-frequency alternating current (thermolysis) or direct galvanic current is delivered to the follicular papilla. This generates targeted micro-heat that coagulates the blood vessels and stem cells responsible for hair growth. Once coagulated, the treated hair slides out smoothly using sterile forceps without any plucking resistance.

## What it can help with

- Scattered coarse grey, white, or blonde hairs on the chin and jawline
- Unwanted hormonal hairs resistant to optical laser hair removal
- Precise eyebrow contouring and clearing unibrow hair strands
- Removing stubborn isolated hairs around the nipples, navel, or hairline
- Perfecting smooth skin following a completed course of laser hair reduction
