## Overview

As the skin undergoes chronological aging and environmental photo-damage, its natural population of fibroblasts becomes less active, producing fewer structural collagen and elastin proteins. This leads to a gradual loss of dermal thickness, dehydration crinkles, and a lacklustre complexion. For patients seeking deep biological skin rejuvenation without introducing synthetic compounds or artificial volumisers, Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) facial therapy offers an advanced, natural alternative.

At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our PRP facial protocols follow strict clinical standards. By drawing a modest blood sample and concentrating your body's own platelets in sterile centrifuge systems, we isolate a potent biological solution packed with natural growth factors. When reintroduced into the dermis, these signaling proteins stimulate blood circulation, encourage cellular repair, and trigger the synthesis of fresh collagen scaffolding. Over subsequent weeks, the skin reveals improved elasticity, smoother texture, and a naturally healthy, rested glow.

## How it works

When concentrated platelets are introduced into the dermis, they release a cascade of essential growth factors, including Epidermal Growth Factor (EGF), Transforming Growth Factor (TGF-beta), and Fibroblast Growth Factor (FGF). These biological messengers bind to cellular receptors on resting fibroblasts, stimulating them to proliferate and synthesise type I collagen and hyaluronic acid. Simultaneously, vascular growth factors promote microscopic capillary growth, restoring nutrient and oxygen delivery to devitalised tissue.
