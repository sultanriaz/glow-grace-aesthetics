## Overview

Melasma is one of the most common and emotionally frustrating pigmentary concerns seen in South Asian dermatology. Characterised by symmetric, grey-brown patches across the cheeks, bridge of the nose, forehead, and upper lip, melasma often emerges following hormonal fluctuations, pregnancy, or chronic sun exposure. In Dera Ghazi Khan, where sunny days and high temperatures predominate, heat and UV rays continuously stimulate overactive pigment-producing cells.

At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, our melasma philosophy avoids harsh, aggressive interventions that risk worsening pigmentation. Because melanin-rich complexions are vulnerable to post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, our doctor designs gradual, multi-phase programmes. We combine gentle in-clinic depigmenting peels, soothing micro-infusions, and clinically calibrated topical regimens that gently suppress tyrosinase activity. With patient education, compassionate care, and consistent photoprotection, we help you achieve a more uniform, balanced skin tone safely.

## How it works

Our melasma treatments target pigment at multiple cellular stages. First, mild keratolytic solutions gently lift melanin-saturated surface cells. Simultaneously, active formulations containing tranexamic acid, azelaic acid, and kojic acid suppress the tyrosinase enzyme within deeper melanocytes, decreasing excessive pigment synthesis. Anti-inflammatory agents calm local dermal vascularity, helping prevent the inflammatory cascade that frequently reactivates pigment production.

## What it can help with

- Bilateral cheek, forehead, and upper-lip hormonal pigmentation (chloasma)
- Persistent dark patches that resist standard cosmetic skin creams
- Sun-induced uneven pigmentation and post-inflammatory darkening
- Restoring skin barrier resilience while calming chronic dermal redness
- Smoothing overall complexion clarity without aggressive flaking
