Melasma presents as irregular, deeper brown or gray patches commonly triggered by sun exposure, hormonal fluctuations, and genetic predisposition. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, our melasma protocols utilize a gentle, conservative clinical approach combining targeted topical suppressants, light exfoliating solutions, and restorative barrier therapies. 

Because aggressive treatments can trigger post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation in melanin-rich skin, our protocols prioritize gradual, controlled pigment modulation over harsh interventions. Patient education regarding strict photoprotection forms an integral cornerstone of each plan. A thorough clinical evaluation by the doctor is required to identify pigment depth and formulate a patient-specific regimen tailored safely to your skin type.
