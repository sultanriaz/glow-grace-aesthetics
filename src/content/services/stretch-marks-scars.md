Stretch marks (striae) and body scars occur when deep dermal layers tear or remodel following rapid tissue expansion, weight fluctuations, or surgical procedures. Glow & Grace Aesthetics offers specialized dermal remodeling therapies that blend advanced microneedling, restorative bio-infusions, and localized skin resurfacing to encourage cellular collagen re-alignment.

While stretch marks cannot be erased completely, clinical treatments aim to significantly soften their visual depth, restore textural elasticity, and bring their color into closer harmony with surrounding healthy skin. An initial clinical consultation allows the doctor to evaluate the age and depth of the scars, setting achievable milestones and outlining an evidence-guided session roadmap.
