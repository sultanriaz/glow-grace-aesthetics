## Overview

In contemporary aesthetic wellness, intravenous micronutrient infusions—popularly known as glutathione or whitening drips—are widely discussed. However, unregulated or unverified salon preparations pose severe health risks. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our approach to intravenous antioxidant therapy is defined by strict medical ethics, patient safety, and clinical oversight. We never treat intravenous therapy as a casual beauty trend.

Our infusions combine high-purity, pharmaceutical-grade reduced glutathione with ascorbic acid (vitamin C) to support systemic cellular detoxification and combat oxidative stress triggered by intense sun, urban pollution, and daily fatigue. Rather than offering unrealistic, rapid alterations in your natural genetic skin tone, our doctor-supervised infusions focus on cellular protection, liver vitality, and overall complexion radiance. Every patient undergoes essential medical screening to ensure physiological suitability before any treatment is initiated, fostering a safe, dignified, and clinical environment.

## How it works

Glutathione is the human body's primary endogenous antioxidant, concentrated within the liver to neutralise reactive oxygen species and free radicals. When administered intravenously, it bypasses digestive breakdown, achieving immediate cellular bioavailability. Within melanocytes, glutathione alters pigment pathways by inhibiting the tyrosinase enzyme and shifting melanin synthesis from darker eumelanin toward lighter, golden phaeomelanin, promoting gradual clarity and uniform luminosity across the skin.
