## Overview

In regions like Dera Ghazi Khan, hot weather, dust, and environmental airborne particles combine with natural facial sebum to create chronic pore congestion, blackheads, and dull, lacklustre skin. Conventional scrubs often cause micro-tears across the delicate facial surface without clearing deep sebum plugs. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, our Carbon and Pico Laser treatments utilise advanced photoacoustic optical physics to achieve profound pore purification and pigment shattering without thermal tissue damage.

Known globally as the 'Hollywood Laser Peel', this procedure pairs an activated medical carbon paste with ultra-fast Q-switched or picosecond laser pulses. The carbon particles penetrate deep into dilated follicular openings, binding tightly to excess oil and keratin debris. When exposed to the laser wavelength, the carbon particles micro-implode, sweeping away trapped contaminants while generating mechanical shockwaves that stimulate fibroblast cells in the upper dermis. The complexion emerges purified, smooth, and visibly brightened, making it a sought-after pre-event treatment with minimal downtime.

## How it works

The treatment operates through photoacoustic and photothermal mechanisms. High-intensity nanosecond or picosecond laser light at 1064 nm is selectively absorbed by the carbon paste and melanin chromophores. The light pulses vapourise the carbon instantaneously, creating localized acoustic waves that shatter superficial debris and micro-fragment dark pigment clusters into microscopic particles. These fragmented particles are subsequently metabolised and cleared through the body's natural lymphatic circulation.
