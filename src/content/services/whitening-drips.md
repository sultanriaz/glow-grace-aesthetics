At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, intravenous antioxidant infusions—often popularly termed whitening or glow drips—are handled with strict clinical rigor and ethical responsibility. Formulations typically combine pharmaceutical-grade antioxidants including glutathione and ascorbic acid (vitamin C) to support systemic cellular detoxification and combat free-radical oxidative stress throughout the body.

We do not promise drastic or instantaneous alterations in natural skin color. Instead, our infusions focus on cellular protection, liver support, and overall complexion radiance. Patient health and safety remain our non-negotiable priority. A thorough clinical evaluation, including a review of medical history and baseline organ function, is mandatory before any infusion protocol can be approved or administered by our medical team.
