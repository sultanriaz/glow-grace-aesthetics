Chemical peels provide a scientifically controlled method of exfoliating weathered, hyperpigmented outer layers of skin to reveal the healthier, smoother epidermis beneath. Using balanced clinical acid formulations including glycolic, salicylic, and mandelic acids, the peel gently loosens cellular bonds to accelerate natural desquamation.

Treatments are strictly calibrated in strength to suit your individual Fitzpatrick skin type, ensuring safety and avoiding unwanted irritation or hyperpigmentation. Following a gentle application, the skin embarks on a regenerative cycle that helps improve tone uniformity. An upfront medical skin assessment determines the appropriate acid concentration and outlines the essential sun protection steps needed throughout the peeling process.
