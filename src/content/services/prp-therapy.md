PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) facial therapy harnesses your body's intrinsic healing biochemistry to revive and remodel aging or fatigued skin. A modest quantity of your blood is collected and spun in a specialized centrifuge to concentrate platelets and vital signaling peptides.

When reintroduced into target dermal layers via precision micro-injections or automated microneedling channels, these concentrated growth factors activate local fibroblasts to synthesize new collagen and glycosaminoglycans. Over subsequent weeks, the skin demonstrates improved moisture retention, finer texture, and restored luminosity. Because the preparation is entirely autologous, allergic sensitivities are virtually eliminated. A clinical consultation with the doctor is required to verify treatment suitability and design an appropriate session sequence.
