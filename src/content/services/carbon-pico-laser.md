Carbon and Pico Laser therapies harness advanced picosecond and Q-switched optical physics to address complexion dullness, stubborn pigmentation, and enlarged pores. During the popular Carbon Laser treatment, a micro-fine layer of medical liquid carbon is applied across the face, settling deeply into follicular pores. 

When passed with the laser, the light rapidly vaporizes the carbon particles, instantly sweeping away trapped sebum, dead cells, and bacteria while stimulating collagen remodeling through gentle photoacoustic shockwaves. Pico laser wavelengths also fragment pigment clusters into microscopic dust that your lymphatic system clears naturally. During your consultation, the doctor will analyze your skin condition to select the optimal laser wavelength and pulse protocol.
