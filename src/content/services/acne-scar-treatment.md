Acne and post-acne scarring require a thoughtful dual-phase strategy: calming persistent active inflammation while systematically remodeling scar tissue beneath the surface. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, our acne scar protocols combine targeted medical peeling, subcision for tethered scars, and collagen-stimulating micro-treatments customized to your unique scar morphology.

Because every skin type experiences scarring differently, treatment plans are tailored to distinguish between rolling, boxcar, and pigmented marks. Our goal is to gradually soften depression depths and restore an even surface texture. An initial clinical consultation with the doctor is mandatory to ensure active infections are stabilized before deeper scar revision techniques commence.
