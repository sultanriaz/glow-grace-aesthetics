## Overview

Skin tags, benign cosmetic moles, and cutaneous warts are frequent sources of cosmetic concern or physical irritation. Moles on the jawline may be nicked during shaving, skin tags on the neck or underarms can catch on clothing fabrics, and viral warts on the hands can cause persistent embarrassment. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, we provide minor clinical procedures to remove these benign lesions safely, hygienically, and with careful attention to aesthetic healing.

Patient safety remains our paramount priority. Every lesion is examined by our doctor prior to any intervention to ensure it displays standard benign features and does not require specialist dermatopathological referral. Once verified, removal is performed using precision radiofrequency or electrosurgical instruments that vapourise or excise the target tissue with microscopic margin control. This approach protects adjacent healthy dermis, minimises bleeding, and encourages rapid re-epithelialisation for a smooth, discreet final outcome.

## How it works

Depending on the lesion type and depth, radiofrequency ablation, electro-dessication, or sterile shave excision is selected. High-frequency electrical energy gently dehydrates and vapourises the cellular structure of the tag or mole layer by layer, sealing micro-capillaries promptly to prevent bleeding. The body then forms a natural superficial protective crust over the treated area, beneath which fresh, unblemished epidermal cells rapidly migrate and close the wound.

## What it can help with

- Unsightly or raised cosmetic moles on the face, neck, and chest
- Irritated or friction-prone skin tags (acrochordons) on the neck and underarms
- Stubborn viral warts and verrucas resistant to over-the-counter paints
- Seborrhoeic keratoses and benign superficial epidermal growths
- Restoring smooth, clean skin surfaces without unnecessary surgical incisions
