Hair transplant procedures at Glow & Grace Aesthetics focus on meticulous follicular unit restoration to help address hereditary thinning and receding hairlines. Following a thorough clinical scalp evaluation, individual follicular units are carefully harvested from healthy donor areas and transplanted into thinning zones with precise alignment to match your natural hair growth direction. 

The procedure aims to restore balanced coverage and facial symmetry over time. Because hair restoration success depends on donor availability, scalp vascularity, and individual healing, every treatment path begins with an individualized medical assessment. Your doctor will determine procedural suitability, discuss realistic density expectations, and create an appropriate pre- and post-procedure care schedule.
