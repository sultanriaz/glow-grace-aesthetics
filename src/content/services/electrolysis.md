Electrolysis remains a gold standard clinical modality for eliminating hairs that cannot absorb optical laser energy—such as gray, white, blonde, or very fine hormonal hairs. Using an ultra-fine, sterile surgical filament inserted directly into the natural opening of each hair follicle, a localized electrical current produces controlled destruction of the follicular regenerative cells.

Once treated, individual hairs slide out smoothly without resistance. Because electrolysis treats one follicle at a time, it offers unmatched precision for shaping brows, clearing scattered chin hairs, or perfecting post-laser results. Our certified practitioners maintain strict sterile conditions throughout. A personalized consultation allows the doctor to inspect hair texture, assess skin tolerance, and plan a comfortable treatment schedule.
