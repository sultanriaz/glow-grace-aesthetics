At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, mole, skin tag, and wart removal is performed with focused clinical precision to prioritize clean healing and aesthetic outcome. Lesions are carefully treated using controlled electrosurgical, radiofrequency, or cryo-therapeutic methods after local numbing ensures patient comfort throughout the brief procedure.

Crucially, any mole that changes in size, shape, or color should be examined by a doctor to rule out underlying atypical pathology before any cosmetic procedure is undertaken. Our medical team conducts an essential clinical examination prior to treatment to confirm lesion safety. Following removal, personalized wound care instructions are provided to promote optimal skin recovery and support smooth, discreet healing.
