The periorbital skin is significantly thinner and more delicate than the rest of the face, making it vulnerable to visible dark circles, sluggish microvascular circulation, and hollow shadowing. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, our under-eye therapies focus on clarifying the underlying physiological causes—distinguishing between genetic pigmentation, vascular congestion, and volume deficit.

Treatment modalities may include targeted micro-infusions of peptides and antioxidants, gentle platelet therapy, or subtle volume restoration. Each approach is calibrated to treat delicate eye contours safely without causing swelling. Because under-eye concerns vary widely in origin, an in-person doctor consultation is necessary to formulate an appropriate, conservative care strategy.
