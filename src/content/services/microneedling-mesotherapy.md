## Overview

Microneedling and mesotherapy represent a powerful clinical combination for revitalising tired, dull, or uneven skin without heat or thermal injury. Over time, sun exposure, environmental pollution, and natural chronological aging reduce collagen production, leading to enlarged pores, fine crinkles, and loss of dermal bounce. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our approach combines automated collagen induction with sterile, pharmaceutical-grade meso-nutrients.

By avoiding thermal heat, microneedling is exceptionally well suited for Asian skin tones, offering collagen rejuvenation while significantly lowering the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation common with aggressive lasers. Every session is conducted in a sterile medical setting using fresh, single-use needle cartridges. Whether you wish to soften shallow acne scars, restore suppleness around the cheeks, or simply boost overall skin health, our doctor-guided protocols provide a measured, gentle path to enduring skin refinement.

## How it works

The automated microneedling handpiece creates thousands of microscopic vertical channels in the epidermis and papillary dermis. These controlled micro-injuries initiate the body's natural wound-healing cascade, stimulating fibroblast activity and producing fresh collagen and elastin. Concurrently, mesotherapy cocktails containing non-crosslinked hyaluronic acid, biomimetic peptides, and vitamins penetrate deeply through these open pathways, providing intense dermal hydration and cellular nourishment that surface creams cannot replicate.

## What it can help with

- Rough, uneven skin texture and enlarged facial pores
- Superficial rolling acne scars and post-blemish indentations
- Fine dehydration lines around the eyes, cheeks, and forehead
- Dull, lacklustre complexion lacking natural vitality and tone
- Mild skin laxity and thinning epidermal barrier resilience
