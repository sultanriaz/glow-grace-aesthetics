## Overview

Natural aging is a multifaceted biological process that alters every layer of the facial architecture. Over time, intrinsic metabolic slowing combined with external ultraviolet exposure leads to the breakdown of essential structural proteins, notably type I collagen and elastin fibres. The skin's dermal matrix thins, cell renewal slows, and moisture retention diminishes, creating crepey texture, fine wrinkles, and subtle loss of firmness across the cheeks, jawline, and neck.

At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our anti-aging philosophy focuses on regenerative vitality rather than artificial alteration. We do not believe in masking your natural character; instead, our doctor designs comprehensive clinical programmes aimed at revitalising your skin's own biological repair mechanisms. By integrating non-crosslinked bio-remodelling hyaluronic acid, cellular polynucleotides, and targeted collagen induction, we help restore healthy dermal thickness, natural suppleness, and a radiant, well-rested glow that matures gracefully.

## How it works

Our anti-aging protocols stimulate neocollagenesis and neoelastogenesis within the deep dermis. Ultra-pure bio-stimulating compounds spread smoothly across receptive interstitial spaces, binding to CD44 receptors on fibroblast cells. This triggers the cellular synthesis of four distinct types of collagen and elastin while drawing essential water molecules into the extracellular matrix. The result is improved dermal density, tightened tissue elasticity, and gradual smoothing of fine static wrinkles.
