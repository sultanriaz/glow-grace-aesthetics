## Overview

Facial injectables at Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan are strictly doctor-performed treatments dedicated to subtle harmony, balance, and natural beauty. Repetitive facial expressions—such as frowning, squinting against bright sunlight, or concentrating—gradually etch dynamic expression lines into the forehead, between the brows, and around the corners of the eyes. At the same time, natural volume loss in the lips can leave the mouth area looking thin, dehydrated, or asymmetric.

Our practice stands firmly against exaggerated, over-filled, or unnatural aesthetics. Every patient undergoes an in-depth clinical consultation with our doctor to examine dynamic facial kinetics and underlying bone structure. By placing conservative micro-doses into precise anatomical vectors, we relax overactive muscle tension and restore delicate hydration and definition to the lips. You leave looking naturally refreshed, well-rested, and confident—like the best version of yourself, with all your authentic facial expressions fully intact.

## How it works

Wrinkle-relaxing neurotoxins work by temporarily intercepting the acetylcholine neurotransmitter signal between motor nerves and specific facial muscles, softening repeated creasing of the overlying skin. Dermal lip fillers consist of sterile, cross-linked hyaluronic acid—a biocompatible sugar molecule naturally found in the skin. When placed beneath the vermilion border and lip body, it binds water molecules, restoring soft volume, refining perimeter borders, and smoothing vertical lip crinkles.

## What it can help with

- Forehead worry lines, glabella frown lines ('11' lines), and crow's feet
- Thin, dehydrated, or age-flattened lip contours
- Asymmetry in the upper or lower lip proportion
- Gummy smile softening and downturned oral commissures
- Restoring a rested, relaxed, and approachable facial expression
