## Overview

Environmental heat, sun exposure, dust, and daily makeup can leave facial skin looking weathered, congested, and prematurely tired. Dead skin cells accumulate on the surface, casting a dull shadow and trapping sebum inside pores. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics in Dera Ghazi Khan, our clinical chemical peel treatments offer a precise, dermatologically supervised method to shed this outer layer and reveal fresh, radiant skin underneath.

Unlike aggressive peels that risk triggering hyperpigmentation in melanin-rich South Asian skin, our clinic specialises in calibrated, progressive peels. Our doctor customises each formula—whether utilising mandelic acid for gentle brightening, salicylic acid for pore decongestion, or glycolic acid for cellular renewal. Every step is timed and observed under medical supervision to ensure comfort and safety. By respecting the natural skin barrier while stimulating fresh cellular turnover, we help you achieve a smooth, bright, and refreshed complexion without lengthy recovery.

## How it works

Chemical peels work through controlled keratolysis. The formulated acid solution breaks the microscopic desmosomal bonds holding dead keratinocytes together in the stratum corneum. As these tired surface cells loosen and shed, the body accelerates basal cell division, drawing fresh, hydrated cells up to the surface. Simultaneously, mild stimulation in the upper papillary dermis encourages collagen synthesis, resulting in refined pores, improved elasticity, and a more uniform light-reflecting surface.

- Superficial sun spots, freckles, and post-acne dark marks
- Fine dehydration crinkles and rough epidermal surface texture
