Laser tattoo removal at Glow & Grace Aesthetics utilizes specialized short-pulse optical lasers to shatter unwanted artificial pigment particles embedded deep within dermal layers. The high-peak energy pulses break down large ink deposits into microscopic particles through intense photoacoustic acoustic waves, allowing the body's natural immune macrophages to engulf and clear the fragments gradually over time.

Because tattoo clearance depends on ink depth, chemical composition, color spectrum, and skin undertone, multiple sessions spaced over appropriate healing intervals are necessary for progressive fading. We prioritize skin preservation to avoid scarring or textural changes. An initial consultation with the doctor provides a realistic assessment of ink responsiveness and outlines a responsible fading timeline.
