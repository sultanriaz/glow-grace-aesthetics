Unwanted facial and body hair can be a source of persistent daily frustration, requiring continuous cycles of painful waxing, threading, or shaving. In Dera Ghazi Khan's warm climate, frequent waxing often results in irritated folliculitis, stubborn ingrown hairs, and post-inflammatory dark marks across delicate facial, underarm, and leg areas. At Glow & Grace Aesthetics, our medical-grade laser hair removal service provides a comfortable, clinically supervised alternative that offers lasting hair reduction.

Safety on melanin-rich Pakistani skin is our highest operational priority. Using calibrated medical laser platforms equipped with advanced contact skin cooling, our clinical team targets follicular melanin while protecting the overlying epidermis. Over a structured sequence of sessions, thick, coarse hairs become visibly finer, slower to emerge, and significantly reduced in quantity. Whether you are managing hereditary facial hair, seeking relief from razor bumps, or preparing for an upcoming wedding, our doctor-guided protocols ensure safe, predictable, and dignified care.

## How it works

The treatment works via selective photothermolysis. Concentrated light pulses at calibrated wavelengths are absorbed by the melanin pigment in the hair shaft and bulb. This optical energy converts into localised thermal heat, raising the temperature of the follicular matrix and dermal papilla to coagulation threshold without heating surrounding dermal tissues. This disables the follicle's ability to regenerate new hair strands during its active anagen growth phase.
